About

I'm Gabriel Reis e Silva, a Senior Technical UI Designer at Halo Studios in Kirkland, WA. Six years in AAA, previously at Electronic Arts on the FIFA / EA FC series.

I love working on UI and exploring shaders to make cool stuff. I'm passionate about learning and about teaching what I learn, most of what I've shipped has been shaped by mentors, and I try to pay that forward on every team I join. Outside of work you'll usually find me deep in a fantasy novel or taking care of my twenty house plants.
